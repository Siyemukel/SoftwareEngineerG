Progress Tips
  • Work 15–20 minutes per lesson.
  • Use flashcards daily for short sessions.
  • Read examples aloud — it helps memory.
  • Break problems into small steps.

Notes for Tutors
Keep instructions short. Use visuals and repeat steps. Allow students to use calculators and manipulatives while learning the steps — focus on understanding, not speed.
